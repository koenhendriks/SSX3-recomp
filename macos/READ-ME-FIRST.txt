SSX 3 recomp for macOS (Apple silicon, macOS 14 or newer)

1. Drag SSX3.app onto Applications.
2. The app is not notarised by Apple. The first time: right-click (Control-click)
   SSX3.app > Open > Open. On macOS 15 and newer, if it is refused:
   System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.
   Or in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/SSX3.app
3. In the launcher press Browse and pick your own SSX 3 (PAL) rip: the folder with
   default.xbe and data/. No game files are included. Then press Play.

Keyboard = player 1. Xbox One / Series and other controllers work over Bluetooth or USB.
F12 marks a problem in the log (with a screenshot).
Source and build instructions: docs/MACOS.md in the repository (GPL-2.0).
